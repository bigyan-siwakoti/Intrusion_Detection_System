- **Campaign 2 — overflow/fuzz payloads:** `175.45.176.3 → 149.171.126.18:21`
  - Filter: `event_type=="ftp" and src_ip=="175.45.176.3" and dest_ip=="149.171.126.18" and ftp.reply test "Login successful"`
  - **59 successful logins** with PASS payloads like `%n%n...`, `%s%s...`, `%99999999s`, `../../../../...`, `AAAAAAAA...` — the stock rule only flagged 34 of these as `FTP PASS overflow attempt`.
- Also: `FTP USER overflow` and `SITE overflow` against `149.171.126.16:21`, where anonymous login and `RETR GEEGAO.Wju` succeeded.

**Outcome:** attacker authenticated repeatedly; servers accepted anonymous/fuzz logins.

### B. SMB IPC$ enumeration sweep — `175.45.176.1 → 149.171.126.11:445`
- Filter: `event_type=="smb" and src_ip=="175.45.176.1" and dest_ip=="149.171.126.11" and smb.command=="SMB1_COMMAND_TREE_CONNECT_ANDX"`
- **58 × `TREE_CONNECT_ANDX` to `\149.171.126.11\IPC$`** over 10:17:09–10:27:31 (~10 min). Stock rule 2102466 fired 133 times (per-session duplicates), but no consolidation existed for this slow sweep.

- No outbound shell was seen **from .16**, so this attempt did not produce an observed shell.

### G. Web application attacks
