Intrusion Detection Systems

Signature-Based
(Misuse Detection)

  • Matches traffic against known attack patterns
  • Deterministic, auditable alerts
  • Low false-positive rate when tuned
  • Cannot detect unknown attacks
  • Requires human-authored rules
Snort, Suricata, ET Open
THIS PROJECT

Anomaly-Based
(Statistical Detection)

  • Flags deviations from baseline behaviour
  • Can detect novel / zero-day attacks
  • High false-positive rates
  • Difficult to interpret or audit
  • Base-rate fallacy problem (Axelsson 2000)
ML classifiers, neural networks

Specification-Based
(Policy Detection)

  • Defines acceptable behaviour via specs
  • Low false-positive rate
  • Some novel-attack capability
  • Labour-intensive specification maintenance
  • Limited practical adoption
Ko et al. 1997, Sekar et al. 2002
