## 6. Coverage gaps identified

1. **Reverse-shell exploit step undetected.** The compromise of `149.171.126.13` was only visible because the shell emitted a `cmd.exe` banner. 2. **SMB IPC$ brute force had no campaign-level detector** — addressed by new SID 7700001.

4. **HTTP long-request fuzzing** (`.2 → .10:80`, 28 events) surfaced only as parser event 2221018; there is no high-fidelity "sustained oversized HTTP request from one source" detector.
5. **Outbound server→attacker `Rothenburg Shellcode`** (`.10:80→.2`, `.16:80→.0`, `.15:80→.2`) could not be confirmed as exploit traffic vs. HTTP-body false positives from alert data alone — worth follow-up with payload/flow inspection.
